## PROJECT PROPOSAL: PROJECT NGX (NVIDIA BLACKWELL EDGE COMMUNITY ACCELERATION)
Sub-title: Democratizing High-Performance AI Infrastructure for Local Communities and Talent Cultivation in Malaysia
## 1. Executive Summary
Project NGX aims to accelerate Malaysia's digital sovereignty by establishing a decentralized, community-shared AI compute network powered by NVIDIA GB10 (Blackwell) NGX-class systems. By deploying these ultra-compact AI supercomputers in authorized local laboratories and residential developer nodes, the project removes the high-cost barrier to sovereign AI engineering. Leveraging the open-source Omnihash GRATIS Grand Unified AI Token Trading Platform, local developers can remotely access, share, and monetize idle compute blocks. This initiative acts as a grassroots multiplier to organically train, upskill, and scale high-tier AI programming talent across the Malaysian population.
------------------------------
## 2. Problem Statement & Strategic Justification

* The Cloud Cost Barrier: High-performance AI hardware (such as NVIDIA's CUDA-native ecosystems) remains financially inaccessible to independent Malaysian developers, student researchers, and early-stage innovators. Reliance on international cloud environments drains local capital and introduces latency.
* Centralization of Infrastructure: Existing AI infrastructure is concentrated inside institutional datacenters. Grassroots programmers in suburban or residential hubs lack proximity to ultra-fast edge processing nodes required for complex LLM fine-tuning and spatial intelligence.
* Sovereign AI Talent Shortage: To meet the targets of the National AI Framework, Malaysia requires a rapid surge in programmers capable of bare-metal optimization, localized distributed training, and edge deployment.

------------------------------
## 3. Project Objectives

* Subsidize & Deploy: Secure matching grants to seed an initial cohort of NGX-class edge hardware nodes across optimized geographic clusters in Malaysia.
* Establish Decentralized Access: Implement the Omnihash GRATIS framework (omnixtar.github.io/h) to turn every hardware unit into a shared web-accessible node, distributing compute allocation peer-to-peer via secure tokenization.
* Train at the Edge: Provide free, low-latency remote computational capacity to local developer circles, directly fostering high-density cohorts of specialized AI talent.

## A. Hardware Profile (NGX Class)
Each node features the next-generation NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip, outputting up to 1,000 TOPS of local FP4 performance alongside unified coherent memory. This specific footprint allows complex 200B+ parameter model loading, fine-tuning, and multi-camera spatial vision computations to execute directly at the edge without cloud dependency.
## B. Allocation Layer (Omnihash GRATIS)
The hardware operates natively with the Omnihash GRATIS Grand Unified AI Token Trading Platform.

* Zero-Overhead Sharing: It utilizes localized cryptographic handshakes and keys via the Omnihash GenKeys structure to provision secure, authenticated remote access to localized developer clusters.
* Tokenized Incentives: Hosts of the hardware (labs/homes) can securely lease compute slices to surrounding neighborhood programmers during off-peak hours using a token-based access system, ensuring 100% hardware utilization.

------------------------------
## 5. Deployment Framework & Site Profiles
To optimize geographic coverage and ensure safety and uptime, the hardware will be allocated across two distinct environmental tiers:

* Tier 1: Authorized Hub Labs. Placed inside community digital hubs, state universities, or co-working spaces to serve as high-visibility testing centers.
* Tier 2: Decentralized Residential Nodes. Granted to vetted, top-tier local AI engineers and system architects. Residential placement eliminates costly industrial real estate overhead, distributing the nodes directly into communities where remote, latency-sensitive developers reside.
