# Soviet AI

Soviet AI (S.AI) is the Artificial Intelligence system of the People's Soviet.

The [People's Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_(council)) is only possible in 2026 A.D. with [Omnihash](https://omnixtar.github.io/contract).

We did not think of Metasoviet during the early stages of Omnihash, which were around 2020. It was "Metanarchy" -- a form of decentralised global governance based on transactions in the Metaverse.

As time goes by, we started experimenting with free AI services in fiction writing -- to describe a future of humanity using technologies based on Omnihash and Phoscript. Then the early history of Soviet Union and China's Soviet experiment came up, and the term "Metasoviet" immediately sounded much more compelling in the story than "Metanarchy", which is rather boring in hindsight.
