# Soviet AI

Soviet AI (S.AI) is the Artificial Intelligence system of the People's Soviet.

The [People's Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_(council)) is only possible in 2026 A.D. with [Omnihash](https://omnixtar.github.io/contract), a decentralised cryptographic contract sheme for people to organise themselves, online or off line, also known as [Metasoviet](https://omnixtar.github.io/h).
